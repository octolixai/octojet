# F2d stage A: identical-prompt reuse on the Spark

Date: 2026-10-01 (timing replay 2026-09-30 22:59-23:14 UTC; production switch 2026-10-01 00:46:43 UTC). Branch
`f2d-stage-a`. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-30-f2d-prefix-reuse-design.md` (section 8 and its amendments).
Every number in sections 2-6 is printed by `python3 results/f2d-stage-a/compute.py` (standard library only) from the
named file in `results/f2d-stage-a/`; section 7 restates its last table, section 8 is transcribed from the operator's
rollout log (not in the directory). Tables below are copied from that output.

## 1. Summary

- Stage A keeps the KV and recurrent state of a finished request in its slot and, when the next request's prompt is
  token-for-token identical, replays the stored first-token logits through the normal sampler instead of prefilling
  (`reuse: "exact"`). Stage B (checkpoints, extension of a prefix) is not part of this deliverable; the `extend` rows
  below are informational.
- The 71,444-token classifier prompt: cold 36.7-39.2 s (F2c's interpolation gives 37.1 s; stage A does not touch the
  cold path), exact hit 4.5-28.3 ms TTFT over 16 gated rows, 5-30 ms as the headline range; replies equal the cold
  reply in every row.
- Gate stage A at `--parallel 3`, GPU exclusive: **FAIL under the original tolerances** (cold spread 4.93 % vs 2 %, one
  warm total 23 ms over its bound), **PASS under the owner's amended tolerances** (cold spread 5 %, +0.1 s transport
  allowance, 2026-09-30). The informational rule "cold total <= T = 30 s" fails (39.1 and 40.0 s), as the spec expects.
- Exactness receipts: 35 stage-A CUDA tests pass, the chunk-size test passes, `bench_openai --expect-equal` 4 arms x 5
  all equal, `concurrent_equal` all equal.
- Known cost: an identical request that arrives while its kept slot is still busy prefills cold a second time (72 s
  TTFT in the acceptance probe). The owner kept classifier traffic on Tower until stage B and a busy-duplicate fix.
- Production: build `f6f4c54` (engine identical to the reviewed `8618113`) switched in at 2026-10-01 00:46:43 UTC with
  3 streams of 262,144 tokens; health, coherence and a repeated short prompt (TTFT 0.138 -> 0.012 s) checked (section 8).

## 6. Consistency with F2c

Method: F2c's clean median TTFTs for the same server configuration (`../f2c-phase1/f2c-phase1-S1-{32k,128k}.jsonl`,
`arm == clean`): 32,000 tokens 15.22 s, 128,000 tokens 68.53 s; linear interpolation in prompt tokens at 71,444
gives **37.12 s**. (F2c ran `--parallel 2`; the cold prefill does not depend on the slot count.)

| cold prefill | TTFT s | minus interpolation s | relative |
|---|---|---|---|
| stream-1 | 37.18 | +0.06 | +0.2 % |
| stream-2 | 39.02 | +1.89 | +5.1 % |
| nostream-1 | 38.05 | +0.92 | +2.5 % |
| nostream-2 | 38.91 | +1.78 | +4.8 % |
| acceptance rep1 | 36.67 | -0.46 | -1.2 % |
| bursts b1-cold | 39.15 | +2.02 | +5.4 % |
| retry P cold | 37.75 | +0.62 | +1.7 % |

Seven cold runs: min 36.67 s, median 38.05 s, max 39.15 s (1,878 tok/s at the median). The measured cold prefills sit
-1.2 % to +5.4 % around the interpolation (the real curve is not exactly linear: attention cost grows faster than
linearly, so a small positive bias is expected), consistent with F2c and with stage A leaving the cold path alone.

## 7. What the classifier would see, and the decision

With the caller's T of about 30 s (a hypothesis the sequential replay does not model: cancellation, overlapping retries):

- The cold call (37.2-39.0 s TTFT; 39.1 / 40.0 s total for 64 tokens) still exceeds T. Stage A cannot shorten it;
  that is F2c Phase 2's target.
- An identical repeat that finds the slot free takes 4.5-28.3 ms TTFT (16 gated rows; 4.6-17.7 ms over the 9 burst
  exact rows), 0.996-1.118 s total for 64 tokens, far under 0.5 T = 15 s.
- An identical request that arrives while its kept slot is still busy costs a second cold prefill: acceptance rep 2
  waited 36.65 s and finished at 72.23 s TTFT; the retry probe's duplicate took 71.42 s. A caller that times out at 30 s
  and retries lands exactly there.

Decision (owner): classifier traffic stays on Tower until stage B and the busy-duplicate fix (defer an identical request
until its kept slot is free, instead of prefilling it again) are in. Stage A is deployed anyway: it is exact (section 2) and takes an identical repeat from 37-39 s to milliseconds (a repeated short prompt from
0.138 to 0.012 s).

## 9. Deviations and raw files

Deviations from the plan:

1. **Container stub tests.** The first correctness run failed 7 host-side script tests by timeout (they fork bash;
   the container's pids limit); fixed in `ca38287` by not running them in the exactness container. The GPU tests passed
   both times. The replay ran on `ca38287`; production runs `f6f4c54` (the commits between them change the gate tolerances, spec and bench scripts; the engine is identical to the reviewed `8618113`).
2. **`--parallel 3`, not the plan's default 2**, to match production; the retry probe's expectation was corrected for
   three slots in `585f9f1` (two slots evict P, a third keeps it). The acceptance server ran at `--parallel 2`
   (`f2d-a-acceptance-server.log`), the timed runs at 3.
3. **Gate amendment.** The first replay failed `cold_spread` and `latency`; the owner amended the tolerances after
   seeing the data (section 4). `f2d-a-gate.json` is the original verdict, `f2d-a-gate-amended.json` the re-run.
4. **Reviewers.** The Codex quota was exhausted during the stage; the task and whole-branch reviews were done by Claude
   subagents (review commit `1dc484f`, reviewed build `8618113`).
5. **Not independently checked:** GPU exclusivity during the replay (operator-attested); T = 30 s is the operator's
   estimate; the manifests are copied in full (2.3 MB total) with their sha256 in `f2d-manifests.out`. No IPv4 addresses, tokens or keys were found in the copied
   logs (the only regex hits were library version strings like `13.1.2.81` in `f2d-exactness*.out`), so nothing was redacted.
