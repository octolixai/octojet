or JIT line in `f2-a2-warm-3-server.log`), so it is recorded as observed.

## Verdict against spec 1.1

(a) Warm starts at most 150 s with the page cache dropped: window 2 gives 138 s and 62 s (window 1's 546 s and
582 s were the mmap read path, fixed) → **met**. (b) Token ids identical across off-1 and the warm starts, every
`draft_equal_all` true: 6 of 6 and 2 of 2 in both windows → **met**. (c) Host memory within 1 GiB of the
baselines: the controller's ruling reads this on VmRSS at healthy against the mean of the two off starts. Computed
from the timeline: off VmRSS mean 33,314,144 kB; warm-4 34,319,184 kB is +1,005,040 kB = +0.96 GiB; warm-3
32,744,556 kB is below it. On VmHWM (off mean 33,452,432 kB) warm-4 is +866,752 kB = +0.83 GiB. Both are under
1 GiB, with a thin margin on VmRSS → **met** on the ruling's reading (see the deviations below).

Deviations from the frozen procedure:

- (i) Window 2's warm starts (warm-3, warm-4) are compared against window 1's cache-off baselines (off-1, off-2),
  not against off starts in the same pause. The conditions were the same (page cache dropped before every start,
  both JIT caches mounted, the same code for the baseline path: the read-path fix changed only `packed_cache.py`,
  which a cache-off start does not use), but the baselines were not re-run in window 2.
- (ii) Spec 3.8 names host peak RSS for criterion (c). The controller's recorded ruling reads (c) on VmRSS at
  healthy (RSS sampled after the checks, not at the healthy instant) against the mean of the off starts: warm-4
  +0.96 GiB. On VmHWM (peak RSS) warm-4 is +0.83 GiB against the off mean and +1,270,232 kB = +1.21 GiB against
  off-2 alone (33,048,952 kB), the smaller baseline.

Qualified verdict: (a) and (b) met as frozen; (c) met on the ruling's reading, over by 0.21 GiB against the smaller
baseline on the frozen peak-RSS reading. Overall: **passes on the ruling's reading of (c)**, with deviations (i)
and (ii) stated.

Discrepancy with the hand-off text: it quoted "off mean 33.45 GB, +0.85 GiB". 33.45 GB is the mean of the off
starts' VmHWM, not VmRSS, and 0.85 is the difference in GB (34.32 - 33.45); in GiB it is 0.83 on VmHWM and 0.96
on VmRSS. The file values are used here and in the spec row.

Note the memory is not lower with the cache: warm-1/2 (the failed read path) sat at 34.9 GB, warm-3 at 32.7 GB and
warm-4 at 34.3 GB, against off 33.0-33.9 GB; the spread between starts of the same kind is about 1 GB.

## Direction after the pass

The owner's direction, relayed by the operator: load time is good enough; the F2 effort moves to prefill speed
(F2c). Adopting the cache in production (`oj-serve`) is a separate proposal.

## Follow-ups

- Affine (MLX) table cache: needs a CPU read/pack path with a CPU/GPU equality proof (spec 3.9).
- About 88 s outside the timed loading region on a first start after a code refresh (warm-3), and in the no-mount timing window:
  cause unconfirmed; check whether the JIT caches are invalidated by the refresh.
- Production rollout proposal: `oj-serve` adopting the cache directory (64G on the host), the triton cache mount
  (production persists `torch_extensions` but not triton), and the first-start build cost (609 s here).
- Window 1 showed the failure only because the page cache was dropped: any future cache read-path change should be
  measured with a dropped page cache.
