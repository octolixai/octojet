## 1. Headline

**What dominates.** At 32k and 128k prefill time is spread over the expert GEMMs and dense work: at 128k the main
phase's largest blocks are `expert_up` 22.1 %, `expert_down` 14.0 %, `hc_readout` 13.2 %, `attn_sparse` 9.0 %,
`idx_scores` 8.1 %, `dense_gdn_in` 7.3 % of the main device total (section 3). At 210k one block takes half the run:
**`idx_select` is 105.3 s of 214.0 s main device time (49.2 %) and 46 % of the 227.0 s TTFT**, against 3.0 s (4.5 %) at
128k and 0.13 s at 32k. The cause is a step, not a smooth quadratic: every `idx_select` call for a block starting at or
above context 131,072 costs about 27.5 ms for full 256-row blocks (the 80-row tail block at 209,920 costs 8.0 ms), the last block below costs 0.89 ms (31x), the cost is flat afterwards, and
97.1 % of the 210k `idx_select` time (102.2 s) sits in the 309 blocks above 131,072 (section 4). nsys shows that
selection is latency-bound: `main:idx_select` has tensor-active 0.05 %, SM issue 2.5 %, SMs active 98.4 % (section 3).
Everything else scales linearly with tokens (ratio 1.0 to 1.2 of last to first quarter ms/row), except `idx_scores`
(6.9x, the expected quadratic) and the small `attn_prep` (2.9x).

**The lever the data selects for Phase 2: L2, exact indexer optimisation, aimed at `_select` above 131,072.**
Growth share of its target buckets is 70.2 % of the 128k-to-210k TTFT increase (rule threshold 20 %), and its own
condition (scoring and selection ms/row last/first quarter >= 1.5x) holds at both sizes (scoring 6.7x / 6.9x,
selection 10.1x / 204.0x). Expected gain, an estimate and not a measurement: if the 3,708 main-phase selection calls
above 131,072 cost what the blocks just below it cost (0.886 ms per call, `idx_select` main device time), 98.9 s of the
105.3 s disappear and the 210k TTFT falls from 227.0 s to about 128 s (about 1,640 tok/s, from 925); the MTP head's
own `idx_select` (8.8 s at 210k) would shrink as well and is not counted. The 210k gate of section 6 (<= 0.90 x
baseline = 204.3 s) needs 22.7 s. The cliff-only fix (blocks at or above 131,072) contributes nothing at 128k, where the prompt
never reaches the wide regime. The whole L2 bucket at 128k (`idx_scores` + `idx_select` + `idx_pool`) is 8.33 s, so
meeting the 128k gate (<= 64 s, -4.53 s) from L2 alone would need a 54.4 % reduction of that bucket: possible in
principle, unproven. A two-lever path is quantified: L1 must save at least 4.53 s, which is 18.6 % of its 24.35 s bucket
(a conditional 20 % reduction gives 63.66 s); L6 likewise needs 4.53 / 21.5 s = 21 %.

**The others against the section 4 rule** (table in section 6): by the rule's own numbers **L1** (larger chunks:
experts + router + plan = 35.5 % of 128k TTFT, condition >= 25 %, median rows per touched routed expert 21 < 100) and
**L6** (dense + HC = 31.4 % of TTFT, condition >= 15 %) also qualify; the rule admits at most two levers in the first
round, so the choice of a second lever, and of whether L1/L6 enter at all, is left to the plan and the owner; this
report does not pick it. L1 and L6 do not address the 210k growth (growth shares 9.6 % and 8.6 %). **L3** (MTP
absorption: removable part 2.7 % of TTFT < 5 %; the whole MTP tree is 3.5 % of TTFT and 6.6 % of the growth, below
rule (a)), **L4** (GDN recurrence 4.3 % < 15 %), **L7** (exposed idle 0.54 % / 0.25 % < 5 %) and **L8** (attention
preparation 0.4 % < 5 %) do not qualify. **L5** cannot enter: sparse attention is 8.6 % (< 10 %) and the GB10's nsys
metric set has no DRAM counters ("counters unavailable"). The section 6 prerequisites are met: clean medians at all
three sizes, kernel data at 128k and 210k, instrumentation overhead <= 0.67 %.

**Conditions of the measurement.** The GPU was exclusive in every window (the owner paused the research training;
`SHARED_GPU=0`, as reported by the operator: the files do not contain an independent check), so these are profiling
baselines at `--parallel 2` (production runs 3; the section 6 gate needs a re-baseline at production's configuration).
Exclusivity is operator-attested: the tenant receipt file (`f2c-phase1-tenants.txt`) predates the windows. Production's
`--parallel` was **2** during the windows (`PARALLEL=2`; production is normally 3) with int8 KV and the packed cache.
The spec requires an exclusive GPU by default; the shared-GPU option was an owner-approved fallback that was not
needed. Mixed build `/tf/flashnext-nvfp4-mixed`
(S1/S2/S3), MLX 4-bit control `/tf/flashnext-mlx-4bit-mtp` (S4), both on the same CUDA engine, code at commit a459985.
Requests sent `max_tokens` 2 and `temperature 0`; drafting on except the arm `S4-128k-nd`.

### 3.6 nsys ranges: GPU busy, launches and metrics, per phase

Source: `f2c-128k-ranges.json` and `f2c-210k-ranges.json` (reducer output at 36b6698, `valid: true`, bounds
`admission`). GPU busy is the kernel-and-copy interval union attributed to each NVTX range through its launching API
calls. Metric columns are percent of peak, means over the range's attributed intervals (`tensor_active_pct`,
`sm_issue_pct`, `sms_active_pct`, `gr_active_pct`, `warps_in_flight_pct`); `dram_*` are null because the GB10's metric
set has no DRAM counters (`counters` field of `preflight-ranges.json`: `no_dram_match`). "timing device ms" is the
in-engine figure from 3.1 to 3.3 for the same block, to show the two instruments agree (busy is mostly within 1 to 5 % of it, and further apart
for sub-second ranges; the nsys arm runs with tracing on). Ranges with no launches (`stage_wait`, `stage_tokens`, `stage_ple_gather`,

no step at 131,072 (1.79 to 1.83 ms per call on both sides) and keeps its smooth growth (2.0, 2.5, 2.8 ms per call in

### 6.2 Lever table (spec section 4)

Rule: a lever enters only if (a) its target buckets are >= 10 % of the 128k clean-median TTFT (68.53 s) or >= 20 % of
the growth share (bucket210 - bucket128) / (TTFT210 - TTFT128) (TTFT difference 158.47 s), and (b) its own entry
condition holds; at most two levers in the first round. Buckets are the main phase's device time, except L3 (the
MTP tree). L3's "removable part" is my mapping of the spec's description (keep: fused input projection, HC readout,
K/V write, indexer keys and pools; skip: scoring and selection, sparse attention and gate, output projection, router,
plan, experts, finish) onto the recorded blocks; it is approximate. L6's "dense + HC" is the five `dense_*` blocks plus
`hc_readout` plus `writeback` (without `writeback` it is 25.6 % and still qualifies). L5's condition cannot be tested.

L1 and L6 also pass the rule's numbers (35.5 % and 31.4 % of the 128k TTFT) and compete for the second slot. L3, L4,
L5, L7 and L8 do not qualify (L5 because its DRAM condition cannot be tested). Savings are not additive and none of

9.6 %; median rows per touched expert 21 < 100), preferred over L6 (31.4 %, 8.6 %) because of the larger bucket and the
M1b proxy. The 128k target (<= 64 s) then needs L1 to save at least 18.6 % of its bucket. Re-baseline at `--parallel 3`
before gating.

## 8. Deviations, incidents and evidence

**Window and configuration**
- `--parallel 2` (the `PARALLEL=2` environment at the windows), not production's usual 3. The spec's 3.3 names "production's `--parallel` at pause time (3 again since 2026-09-30 04:30 UTC)"; the scripts took it from `PARALLEL`, and the windows ran with 2. `f2c-phase1-S*-args.txt`, `serve-args.txt`.
- GPU exclusive in every window (`SHARED_GPU=0`; the research training paused; production and the Qwen 27B tenant stopped by the pause script and restored afterwards: "PRODUCTION RESTORED" at 12:20:39, 12:26:52, 12:38:46 and 12:59:18 UTC). The spec requires an exclusive GPU by default (a shared GPU only as an owner-approved fallback, labelled "shared GPU"); exclusivity was not needed to be waived. These are profiling baselines at `--parallel 2`, not gate baselines: the section 6 gate needs a re-baseline at production's configuration. `f2c-phase1-tenants.txt` (the preflight's `nvidia-smi` listing, taken beside production, not in a window) shows the pre-window state only.
- The MLX 210k control was run (one clean arm, 231.39 s) because the receipt at `--parallel 2` admits 262,144 tokens a stream; the spec's section 6 had excluded it on the `--parallel 3` capacity of 154,444 tokens.
- The runner used `max_tokens` 2, `temperature 0`, drafting on except `S4-128k-nd`.

**Capture-range-end change** (spec 3.2, commit 7965ba3). The first captures used `--capture-range-end=stop-shutdown`, which terminated the application at `cudaProfilerStop()`: the profile arm's SSE stream was cut (`RuntimeError: the stream ended without a final chunk carrying usage/stats`; the failed row is in `f2c-phase1-S2-210k.jsonl`, rep 1, and is ignored by the computations) and the pause script aborted before reduction. The fix is `--capture-range-end=stop` (`nsys-flags.env`, `serve-args.txt` for the flags and arguments), one capture per server launch, the server then stopped with a grace period.

**Pauses and re-runs** (UTC, 2026-09-30)
- Pause A, 11:44 to 12:20: S1 complete (clean and timing arms at all three sizes; histogram arms at 128k and 210k only); S2 capture written, profile arm cut as above; production restored 12:20:39. `pause-a-console.txt`.
